# 04 — Catalog, naming, units, and Suppliers

**What to build:** Pharmacy staff can manually maintain the products and Suppliers available in their country, search them in Arabic and English, and use safe package/unit definitions without changing historical records.

**Blocked by:** 02 — Identity, Tenant, roles, and permissions.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Staff can create pharmaceutical and general product records with structured naming fields and independent Arabic search names.
- [ ] Product identity uses stable application identifiers and approved SKU/barcode/registration constraints rather than generated display names.
- [ ] Staff can define Inventory Units, package conversions, barcode aliases, and versioned naming templates.
- [ ] Suppliers can be created, searched, archived, and associated with purchasing without destructive deletion of referenced history.
- [ ] Product, unit, name, barcode, and Supplier behavior is covered by multilingual search, conversion, uniqueness, and snapshot tests.
