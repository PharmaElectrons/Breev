# 08 — Returns, reversals, corrections, and approvals

**What to build:** Authorized staff can correct posted business events through linked returns, reversals, and replacements while preserving the original invoice and enforcing step-up or dual-control rules for high-impact actions.

**Blocked by:** 07 — POS sales and continuous Cash Box.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A return links to the original sale where available and creates its own stock, refund/debt, journal, audit, and printable evidence.
- [ ] A reversal creates a complete linked offset and leaves the original Pharmacy Sales Invoice visible and immutable.
- [ ] A replacement can be created after reversal without rewriting prior business facts.
- [ ] No-invoice returns, exceptional discounts, backdating, and other high-risk actions require named permission, reason, evidence, re-authentication, and current-state validation.
- [ ] Stale approvals are rejected, self-approval is impossible for dual control, and all outcomes are auditable.
